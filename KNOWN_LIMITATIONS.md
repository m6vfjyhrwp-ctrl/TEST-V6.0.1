# Known Limitations — v5.1.1

- The database contains 104 production records across nine implemented record types; it is not a complete representation of every collectible category in the game.
- Published treasure-map totals conflict across current guides, so universal treasure-map completeness is not certified.
- Existing records retain their production verification labels for compatibility, but physical gameplay verification was not performed during this release.
- Map coordinates and calibration were statically validated, not compared marker-by-marker on a physical game map.
- Physical iPhone/iPad PWA testing remains pending.
